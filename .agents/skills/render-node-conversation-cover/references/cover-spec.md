# Conversation cover specification

- Master output: PNG, 1600 × 800 px, exact 2:1 landscape.
- Keep the outer 6% clear of essential face, hands, and mission props.
- Default camera: eye-level medium or medium-close, 35–50 mm equivalent.
- Use one featured conversation partner by default. Show one concrete action and at most one supporting prop.
- For a group-introduction Node or an explicit user request for an ensemble, include 3–5 named, canon-approved characters when the lesson supports it; keep one focal partner and a clear social action.
- Compose naturally around the middle. Connected action and eyelines matter more than exact centering; preserve calm overlay space when practical.
- Represent the learner through camera position or a neutral foreground object, not a visible avatar by default.
- Keep backgrounds minimal and low-contrast: one recognizable location cue, broad calm shapes, and soft depth. Characters and their interaction must dominate.
- Use only one or two essential supporting props in a typical cover; for an explicitly requested activity scene, include only the fewest items needed to make the activity legible.
- Avoid decorative clutter such as collages, packed shelves, multiple boards, posters, dense signage, many small objects, and prop clusters. Avoid crowds, landmark collages, and readable generated text.
- Artwork only: no title, captions, labels, logos, UI, decorative frame, watermark, or pseudo-text.
- At card size (about 240 × 120 px), the character, expression, and action should remain clear.
- Use the actual Node scenario, objective, dialogue, and role constraints to choose the cover moment; the title alone is not enough.
