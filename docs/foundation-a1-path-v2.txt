Banana English Foundation A1 Path V2

Content-only redesign. Uses existing session types only: lesson, emoji speak,
say it in English, describe it, explain it, mission. Emoji Speak is for single
noun/verb vocabulary; Say It is Thai-to-English sentence retrieval. Describe It
and Explain It appear only after the relevant vocabulary is taught. Missions
have three communication goals, accept packed answers, and close on completion.

Mission rules: semantic checkpoints, not one fixed grammar answer; packed answers
can satisfy multiple goals; do not introduce untaught vocabulary; close after
goals are complete or turn limit; never ask an extra question after completion.

A1 coverage: personal information, greetings, family, pronouns, possession,
numbers, money, time, days, colors, objects, food, likes/dislikes, wants/needs,
daily actions, can/can't, politeness, survival English, questions, places,
directions, weather, shopping and closing conversations.
