BANANA ENGLISH — FOUNDATION A1 PATH V4
HYBRID VALIDATION ROADMAP (CONTENT ONLY)

Goal
Keep the early lessons that have proven learner feedback. Test the more
granular, Nintendo-style Ki → Sho → Ten → Ketsu pacing only in later content
where the team has less evidence. Do not change mechanics, schemas, scoring,
or live node IDs in this phase.

Validation principle
- “Legacy core” means retain the current lesson’s scope and teaching script as
  the default. We can improve copy or examples, but do not split it yet.
- “Experimental arc” means a new A1 gap or a later, lower-risk topic where we
  deliberately test smaller lessons + retrieval + a short payoff.
- Mission: exactly three learner goals, only previously taught language, and
  close the conversation as soon as all goals are satisfied.
- Emoji Speak: individual words only. Say It: sentences only.

CHAPTER 1 — FIRST CONVERSATION [LEGACY CORE: KEEP]
1  [Lesson] Greetings
   Keep existing scope: Hello / Hi / Good morning / afternoon / evening.
2  [Lesson] Introductions
   Keep existing progression: name → Nice to meet you → country → city/work.
3  [Say It] Introduce Yourself
4  [Lesson] Yes / No / Maybe
5  [Mission] First Conversation — greet, name, country. Three replies.

Why keep it: Greetings and Introductions are the best-validated onboarding
content. Splitting them creates more nodes without a demonstrated learner gain.

CHAPTER 7 — THINGS AROUND ME [EXPERIMENTAL ARC 1]

   chunks, not as grammar terminology.
Sho
52 [Emoji Speak] Things Around Me
   bag, apple, book, shirt, shoes — words are taught in node 51/examples.

   This is a bag. / I have two books. / It’s an apple.
Ketsu

Research question: do learners find one compact “one/more things” lesson clearer
and less repetitive than separate A/An and plural lessons?

CHAPTER 10 — LIGHT CONSOLIDATION
63 [Emoji Speak] Foundation Mix
   Five random words, all from previous content; never introduce new vocabulary.
64 [Mission] Foundation Final
   Learner chooses one familiar context: meet someone, shop, ask for help,
   talk about family, or find a place. Exactly three known sentences.

RELEASE / REVIEW PLAN
1. Ship chapters 1–6 unchanged in scope; they remain the baseline.
2. Build chapters 7–9 as the first granular-content experiment.
3. Compare completion, repeat attempts, time per node, and qualitative rating
   for chapters 7–9 against the legacy chapters.
4. Only split an older proven lesson if the experiment demonstrates a clear
   learner benefit. Otherwise keep the proven broader script.

Expected shape
64 core nodes. The start remains familiar and low-risk. The granular test begins
after the learner already understands the app and has enough vocabulary to feel
the payoff of smaller A1 arcs.
